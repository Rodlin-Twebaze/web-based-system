Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does it take to resolve a complaint?
    • - Most Complaints are resolved within 30 days, but complex cases may take longer.
  • Is my information private?
    • - Yes, we take privacy seriously. Your personal information will not be shared without your consent.
  • Can I submit a complaint anonymously?
    • - Yes, you can submit a complaint without providing your personal information, but it may limit our ability to follow up with you.
  • What types of complaints can I submit?
    • - You can submit complaints related to public services, corruption, infrastructure issues, and other civic concerns. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us address your complaint effectively.
